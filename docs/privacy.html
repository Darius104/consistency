Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

Who this is

Consistency is built and run by one person, not a company. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and what choices you have about it.

What's collected

  • Account info: your email address, used to sign in and identify you.
  • App content: the tasks, templates, completions, streaks, and notes you create.
  • Profile info: a display name, optional bio, and avatar choice, shown to friends you connect with.
  • Friend connections: if you link with another user, that connection, and any short notes you send each other.
  • Support tickets: anything you send through the in-app support/help form.

No location data, contacts, camera, or microphone access is ever requested or used.

Why it's collected

Purely to run the app: sync your data across your own devices, let you view a friend's calendar if you've both agreed to connect, and let you reach out if something's broken. Nothing here is used for advertising, and nothing is sold to third parties.

Where it's stored

Your data lives in a Supabase-hosted Postgres database (Supabase is a third-party cloud infrastructure provider - see their own privacy policy for how they handle infrastructure-level data). Access to your own data is restricted by row-level security rules, so no other user can read your data except a friend viewing your calendar, which you explicitly enable by connecting with them.

Your choices

  • Export a copy of your data any time from Settings → Account → Backup.
  • Remove a friend connection at any time from Settings → Friends.
  • Delete your account and everything tied to it from Settings → Account → Delete account - this is permanent and immediate.

Children's privacy

Consistency isn't directed at children, and knowingly collecting data from children under 16 isn't intended. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and it will be removed.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated as the app changes. The date at the top always reflects the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data? Reach out at comandarius@gmail.com.
